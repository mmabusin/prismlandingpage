    The standing

    One score for every player, against his own position.

    Each player is measured against everyone in his position in his own league, on the things that matter for that position, then ranked from 0 to 100 with 50 the middle. A winger is judged as a winger, a centre back as a centre back.

    • Like for like. Eight position groups, each with its own measures, from duels and aerials for centre backs to shots and dribbles for wide players.
    • One number everywhere. The same standing on every player page, ranking, search and answer, always with his rank beside it.

        From the founder

        Why I built this.

        I love football. That's the honest starting point, and I don't have a cleverer version of it.

        Proper scouting tools exist, and they're very good, but they're built and priced for clubs. If you think seriously about the game without a recruitment department behind you, you get a stats table and you're left to work the rest out yourself.

        The Prism is my go at closing that gap. You don't need to know which stats to pull or how to weigh them. You ask the question you'd actually ask, and it does the digging.

        I also wanted it to be fun. Turning up a 19-year-old you've never heard of who's quietly out-producing top players should feel great. Most tools manage to make that feel like admin.

        Founder, The Prism

        Questions

        Good to know

        What is The Prism?

        An AI-powered football scouting and analytics platform for the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, the Bundesliga and Ligue 1. Every match, player and club has its own page, and you can ask The Prism anything about them in your own words.

        Do I need to know much about stats?

        No. Ask the way you'd ask anyone. If an answer uses a measure you haven't come across, ask what it means and The Prism will tell you.

        Does it make things up?

        It's built not to. Numbers about players, clubs and matches come from live match data, and the figures in an answer are checked against that data before you see them. When a player has few minutes, the answer says so.

        What is the standing?

        The Prism's one score for a player: how he compares with everyone in his position in his own league, on that position's measures, from 0 to 100 with 50 the middle. It always comes with his rank, such as 1st of 28 wide players in the Bundesliga.

        What does it cost?

        You can start on the free plan with no card: it has all of the data and a few questions to The Prism AI each week. Plus and Pro are paid plans, monthly or yearly, with more questions every week. The plans page has the prices and what each one includes.

        How do I sign up?

        With Google or your email at app.theprismai.com. You're in straight away.

        What happens to my email?

        We use it for your account and the occasional email about The Prism, and you can turn those off whenever you like. The privacy policy has the details.
